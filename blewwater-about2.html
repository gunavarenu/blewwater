How Blew Water Began

Blew Water started with two college friends, Hrushikesh Jog and Shivraj Karle, who realized that even after maintaining healthy diets, many people still lacked essential vitamins required for daily wellness.

Their journey initially began with eco-friendly bottle concepts focused on sustainability. While exploring healthier lifestyle solutions, they discovered a bigger opportunity — combining hydration and nutrition through vitamin-enriched beverages.

That vision eventually evolved into Blew Water, a refreshing wellness-focused brand designed for modern living.

Wellness. Hydration. Innovation.

Inspired by the emotional simplicity of everyday refreshment and powered by futuristic wellness thinking, Blew Water merges hydration with intentional nutrition. Every bottle represents balance — healthier lifestyles wrapped inside refreshing experiences.
