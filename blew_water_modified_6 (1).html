Meet the Memory

That boy who waited all year
for summer vacation.

School uniform still on. Mud on the knees. That water bottle swinging by his side. The moment the last bell rang, he would run — not home, but to the mango tree.

We made Blew Water for that boy. For the afternoons that smelled of dust and mango leaves. For the summers that never really ended — they just waited inside us, quietly.

Our Story

Remember that Guava wala bhaiya
outside your school gate?

School bags dropped at the gate. Afternoon light stretching long and golden. That feeling of biting into a fresh guava with salt and masala — spicy, tangy, perfect — and the world making complete sense.

We built Blew Water to bring that back. Not nostalgia in a bottle, but the real thing — refreshing guava goodness, vitamins, electrolytes — nothing you do not need, everything childhood taught you to love.
